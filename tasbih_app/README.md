# تسبيح

A realistic prayer-bead (misbaha) app for Android, with home-screen widgets.

- **Beads:** flick the lowest bead down a little and let go. It slides down the string under
  gravity and knocks into the counted beads, with a click, a short echo and a vibration
  (`lib/ui/bead_string.dart`).
- **Tasbihat:** Tasbih al-Zahra (الله أكبر 34، الحمد لله 33، سبحان الله 33) plus common adhkar.
  You can add your own or change counts. Progress is saved per tasbih (`lib/data/`).
- **Streak:** a day counts when it has at least 33 beads.
- **Widgets:** phrase of the day (4×2), counter (2×2) and verse of the day (2×2). The app saves
  its state and the phrase lists. The widgets pick the phrase for the day themselves, so it
  changes after midnight without opening the app (`android/app/src/main/kotlin/.../widget/`).

## Build

CI (`.github/workflows/tasbih-apk.yml`) builds a signed APK and publishes it as the
`tasbih-latest` release:
<https://github.com/Malek-hamdan/my-new-video/releases/download/tasbih-latest/tasbih.apk>

Locally: `flutter test` and `flutter build apk --release`.

The click sounds are generated by `python3 tool/gen_sounds.py assets/sounds`. It needs numpy and scipy.
